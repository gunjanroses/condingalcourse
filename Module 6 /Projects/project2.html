Our story

Rooted in tradition,
made for today.

We started Saffron & Stone with a simple idea: the most memorable meals are the ones that feel both surprising and familiar. Our kitchen brings together bright regional flavours, handmade breads, and the generous spirit of Italian hospitality.

Everything is cooked to order, shared at the centre, and served with a smile.
